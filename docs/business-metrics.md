# Metrics and outcomes

## Technical metrics

Execution success/failure/timeout counts, execution duration, per-state failures, Bedrock calls,
bounded retries, allergy-risk classifications, and high-priority classifications are observable from
Step Functions and EMF metrics.

## Business KPIs

Potential KPIs are allergy-related escalation rate, support contacts per order, payment/logistics
reconciliation defects, notification delivery rate, dispatch lead time, and customer churn. The
reference stack does not contain the business data or experimental design needed to measure them.

## Projected benefits

Earlier risk detection and structured sentiment may reduce manual triage and unsafe handling. Those
are hypotheses. Validate with a baseline, controlled rollout, human review, and confidence intervals.

## Measured results

No production business outcome has been measured. Local test pass counts and simulator latency are
technical development results only and must never be presented as business impact or AWS benchmark.
