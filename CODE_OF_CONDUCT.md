# Code of Conduct

Participation requires respectful, professional collaboration. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable. Technical disagreement should
focus on evidence, trade-offs, and the safety of users.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the verified contact on their
GitHub profile. The owner may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe and constructive project environment.
